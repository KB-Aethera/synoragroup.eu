Synora Group is the institutional and research origin of the wider Synora programme.

Its first public disclosure introduced NOUS — a new class of weightless autonomous intelligence designed beyond trained parameters and the assumptions of the dominant model paradigm.

The work has since expanded. Original theory, native computation, extreme-speed inference and external intelligence systems are now converging through Synora Labs.

The original thesis

Intelligence should not have to be fully encoded before it encounters the world. NOUS began from a refusal of fixed trained intelligence: the system should derive its operative form from what it actually encounters.
